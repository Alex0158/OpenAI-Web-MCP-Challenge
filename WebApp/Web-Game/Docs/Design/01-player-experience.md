# Player Experience

**Status:** Working design

## First session

The player wakes at a small shelter with a turret, a 24-tile resource-sensing field, five starter soldiers,
and a partially hidden world map. Any starting coin balance follows the accepted onboarding rule;
the first useful action is to assign a simple gathering or hunting mission and watch the world
continue.

## Ongoing play

The player alternates between direct exploration and shelter command. Exploration reveals new routes,
resources, monsters, and enemy shelters. Shelter command turns findings into missions, upgrades,
recalls, migration, or siege preparation. The player can close the page while missions continue.

The dashboard treats a loss as a readable causal report. It tells the player what mission was active,
what tool and cargo were involved, where the encounter occurred, what killed the soldier, what was
lost, and which decisions remain available.

## Choice quality

The game should reward route and risk selection rather than repeated button presses. A player weighs
travel time, yield, tool tier, detection range, cargo capacity, monster pressure, enemy activity,
return safety, and the cost of exposing a shelter.
