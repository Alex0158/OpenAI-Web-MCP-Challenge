# Magic and Lore

**Status:** Working lore baseline  
**Canon boundary:** Fictional explanation only; gameplay rules remain in `Mechanics/`

## Shelter magic

Every shelter is built around a magical core. The core gives the settlement a sensing field, a home
anchor for soldiers, a controlled resurrection point, and the command authority to assign missions.
The magic does not make an outdoor soldier invincible; it only enables the shelter-based return and
respawn relationship.

## Veil migration

A shelter can invoke a veil while it migrates. The veil gradually removes the settlement from the
sight of new observers. Other players retain the shelter's last known position and can still act on
old intelligence, but they cannot obtain a fresh position while the veil is active. The veil consumes
one charge, recharges automatically after its cooldown, and can be supplemented by an expensive
purchased charge.

The veil is concealment rather than a retroactive undo. A battle already committed at the shelter
continues according to the server's event order. The turret is silent during migration and the
settlement cannot dispatch a new field mission until it arrives.

## Broken protection and corruption

When a shelter is breached, its magic can no longer protect soldiers that are outside. Those field
soldiers lose their human command link and become roaming monsters in the shared world. They are no
longer controlled by their former player. Soldiers that were inside remain associated with the
shelter's damaged core and can help the player recover.

## Tone

The world should feel dangerous, legible, and persistent. A loss creates a visible history and a
new world actor. Magic explains mobility and re-entry without hiding the underlying authority model:
the server still owns every state transition.
