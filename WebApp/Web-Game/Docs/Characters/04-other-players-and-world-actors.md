# Other Players and World Actors

**Status:** Working concept baseline

Other players own shelters, player avatars, soldiers, intelligence records, and mission histories.
Their locations and shelter visibility are governed by fog, sensors, migration, and last-known-position
rules. A player can discover another shelter personally, report it to the home shelter, and launch a
siege against a time-stamped coordinate.

The shared world also contains resource nodes, generated monsters, corrupted soldiers, cargo left by
future mechanics, and terrain obstacles. All actors use the server's ownership and visibility rules;
the browser never infers ownership from a visual label alone.
